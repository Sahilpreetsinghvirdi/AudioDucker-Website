Who is this for?

Built for people who want to stay in flow without touching a single button.

The Student

Studying a course on YouTube? Keep Spotify on underneath — the music dips to a quiet level so the instructor stays crystal clear, and comes back when you pause.

The Vibe Coder

Playing a tutorial while you build? Audio Ducker settles your playlist behind the video. Pause it — and your music is right back, full volume, no missed beat.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing to configure after setup.

01

Play the lecture

Start any course or video in your browser. Audio Ducker watches the browser's audio session — the same one the volume mixer uses.

02

Music dips low

Your chosen apps fade smoothly to a quiet background level. The instructor stays front and center while your playlist keeps playing.

03

Pause, it returns

Hit pause and your music glides right back to its exact volume. No resetting, no fiddling — ever.

Instant Detection

          
Audio Ducker sits quietly in your system tray. The moment your browser starts making sound, your music fades. When it stops, everything returns to your exact volumes — even if you adjusted them mid-duck.

          

            
Works with Spotify, VLC, YouTube and more

            
Zero configuration required

            
Restores automatically when the video stops

          

        

        

          

            
          

          
Universal Compatibility

          
Detects the browser's audio session automatically. Add an optional companion extension for even more precise YouTube-tab detection.

        

        

          

            
          

          
Privacy First

          
No recordings, no telemetry, no cloud. Audio Ducker only watches which apps are making sound — nothing ever leaves your PC.

        

      

    

  


  

    

      
Lightweight by design

      
Engineered to run unnoticed in the background. Audio Ducker uses negligible resources, so your CPU stays where it belongs — your course, your code, your game.
