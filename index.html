Features

The study-flow companion: lectures first, beats second.

Made for learning

Watch a course or lecture while Spotify keeps playing underneath. Music dips low, never stops, and surges back when you pause.

Smooth fades

Volume glides down and back up over a duration you choose — no abrupt drops that yank you out of the flow.

Pick your apps

Choose which apps duck (Spotify, VLC, games...) or duck every other app whenever you're watching something.

Restores to your levels

Every app is restored to the exact volume it had before, even if you adjusted it mid-duck.

Precise browser detection

Optional companion extension tells Audio Ducker exactly how many YouTube tabs are playing, so it ducks only when it should.

Quiet and tidy

Sits in the system tray. Right-click for settings. Logs to a file for diagnostics — no telemetry, no cloud.

Install in one command

Copy this into Windows PowerShell (or any terminal) and press Enter:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sahilpreetsinghvirdi/AudioDucker/main/install.ps1 | iex"

This downloads the latest release to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\AudioDucker and starts it. Or grab the zip from the Releases page and run AudioDucker.exe.

How it works

Three steps between you and your study playlist.

STEP 1

Right-click the tray icon and open Settings. Tick your music apps — Spotify, VLC, anything you jam to.

STEP 2

Pick the background volume (e.g. 25%) and how fast the music dips and rises.

STEP 3

Play the lecture. Music settles low behind the instructor. Pause — the music comes roaring back.

Frequently asked questions

So I can listen to music while studying a course?

Exactly that. Keep Spotify (or any player) on, start the lecture, and Audio Ducker lowers your music to a background level so you hear the instructor clearly. Pause the video and your music fades back up to full volume.

What makes it duck? Does it listen to my microphone?

No. Audio Ducker watches the browser's audio session — the same Windows mechanism the volume mixer uses. It knows when the browser is making sound and acts on that. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Can I control exactly which apps get ducked?

Yes. In Settings you can pick specific apps, or enable "duck all others" to quiet everything else while you play something in the browser.

What happens if I change the volume while it's ducking?

Your manual change is respected. Audio Ducker re-reads the baseline and restores apps to the levels you set.

Is there a browser extension?

An optional extension makes browser detection precise — it tells the app how many YouTube tabs are playing so it only ducks when a tab is actually audible. See the repo's browser/ folder for install steps.

How do I uninstall?

Close the app from the tray and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\AudioDucker. Settings live in %APPDATA%\AudioDucker.

Is it really free?

Yes — free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license. You can even build it yourself from the source on GitHub.
